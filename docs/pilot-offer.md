# Evidence Defensibility Sprint

## Offer

A two-week review sprint for IT GRC, Internal Audit, TPRM, Privacy, SDLC, IAM, and AI Governance teams.

## Deliverables

- Evidence defensibility review
- Findings register
- Evidence request list
- Control Atlas mapping
- Board-ready summary
- Closure readiness tracker
- Remediation action plan

## Best buyer

Teams that already have evidence but are unsure whether it will survive audit, management review, 2LOD credible challenge, or examiner scrutiny.

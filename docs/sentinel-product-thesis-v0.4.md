# Sentinel Product Thesis

## Positioning

Sentinel is an AI-native assurance intelligence platform for regulated organizations.

It does not merely store evidence. It challenges evidence.

## First commercial wedge

Metrics and Evidence Examiner for IT GRC, audit, and 2LOD credible challenge teams.

## Buyer pain

Regulated organizations often have dashboards, trackers, screenshots, decks, and evidence packages. What they lack is a consistent examiner-style review of whether the evidence actually proves:

- source lineage
- denominator accuracy
- reporting-period consistency
- reviewer approval
- control operating evidence
- vendor reliance logic
- privacy/data handling defensibility
- SDLC governance completion
- AI governance readiness

## Differentiation

Most GRC tools are repositories and workflow trackers. Sentinel acts as an assurance intelligence layer.

## Near-term monetization

Use Sentinel as a consulting accelerator:
1. Evidence Health Check
2. IT Metrics Validation Review
3. TPRM Evidence Challenge
4. AI Governance Readiness Review
5. SDLC Governance Evidence Review

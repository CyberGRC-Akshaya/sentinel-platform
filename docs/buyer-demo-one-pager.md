# Sentinel Buyer Demo One-Pager

## Product

Sentinel Evidence Defensibility Workbench

## Company

Eye On Bits Pvt Ltd

## Positioning

Sentinel is an evidence defensibility challenge layer for audit, risk, compliance, TPRM, privacy, SDLC, IAM, and AI governance reviews.

## Problem

Teams often collect evidence, but the evidence may still be weak:

- screenshot-only
- missing source lineage
- missing reviewer approval
- unclear reporting period
- inconsistent denominator or calculation logic
- weak mapping to control objectives
- missing data-flow or privacy context
- missing CUEC or SOC 2 reliance analysis
- unclear closure evidence

## Solution

Sentinel reviews evidence packages and produces:

- evidence defensibility score
- findings
- control atlas mapping
- missing evidence list
- evidence request messages
- closure readiness scores
- board-ready summary
- remediation register
- delivery checklist

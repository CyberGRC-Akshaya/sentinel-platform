## Problem Section

Regulated organizations often prepare evidence packages for audits, risk committees, vendor reviews, security governance, privacy reviews, and AI governance.

But evidence packages frequently fail under deeper scrutiny because they do not clearly prove:

- source lineage
- calculation logic
- denominator completeness
- reviewer approval
- reporting-period consistency
- SOC 2 reliance logic
- customer data processing boundaries
- release governance completion
- AI approval and monitoring evidence

## Solution

Sentinel acts as an assurance intelligence layer that reviews evidence and generates:

- examiner-style findings
- evidence gaps
- challenge questions
- remediation guidance
- framework relevance
- report-ready outputs

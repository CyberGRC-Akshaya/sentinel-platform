[Client/Business Unit] is seeking assurance over selected evidence packages used for governance, audit, risk, vendor, privacy, SDLC, IAM, or AI governance purposes.

## 2. Objective

The objective of this review is to assess whether the selected evidence package is sufficiently complete, traceable, and defensible to support management claims and withstand audit, 2LOD, or examiner-style scrutiny.

## 3. Scope

The review will cover:

- evidence completeness
- source lineage
- management conclusion support
- metric calculation logic, where applicable

- privacy/data handling evidence, where applicable
- SDLC/AI/IAM governance evidence, where applicable
- remediation evidence requirements

## 4. Approach

The review will use the Sentinel Assurance Platform as an accelerator and expert review methodology to generate:
- examiner-style findings
- evidence gaps
- challenge questions
- remediation requests
- framework mapping rationale
- finding register

All outputs will be reviewed and refined by Eye On Bits before client delivery.

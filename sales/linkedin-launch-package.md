# LinkedIn Launch Package

## Post 1 — Build-in-public launch

I have started building **Sentinel** under Eye On Bits Pvt Ltd.

Sentinel is an AI-native assurance intelligence console for regulated environments.

The first module focuses on a problem I have repeatedly seen in IT GRC, audit, vendor risk, privacy, SDLC governance, and AI governance:

**Evidence exists, but it does not always prove what management claims it proves.**

Sentinel reviews sample evidence packages and generates:

- assurance score
- evidence gaps
- examiner-style challenge questions
- remediation guidance
- severity distribution
- top risk domains
- downloadable examiner report

Initial use cases:
- IT metrics validation
- vendor / SOC 2 reliance review
- privacy and customer data evidence review
- SDLC release governance review
- AI governance evidence readiness

This is not meant to replace professional judgment. It is meant to make assurance review sharper, faster, and more defensible.

Building this publicly and refining it step by step.

## Post 2 — Problem-led post

A common issue in regulated environments:

Teams collect evidence, prepare dashboards, and submit governance reports.

But when challenged by audit, 2LOD, or regulators, the hard questions are different:

- What is the source of record?
- Is the denominator complete?
- Does the reporting period align?
- Who reviewed the evidence?
- Does the SOC 2 report actually cover the relied-upon controls?
- Where is the CUEC analysis?
- Where is the data-flow evidence?
- Where is the AI use case approval record?

That is the problem Sentinel is being built to solve.

Not evidence storage.

Evidence challenge.

# LinkedIn Launch Post Draft

I am building Sentinel by Eye On Bits Pvt Ltd.

Sentinel is an AI-native assurance intelligence console designed for regulated environments where evidence quality matters as much as control design.

The first module focuses on a practical problem I have seen repeatedly in governance, audit, risk, and security programs:

Evidence exists, but it does not always prove what management claims it proves.

Sentinel v0.4 can review sample evidence packages and generate:
- assurance score
- evidence gaps
- examiner-style challenge questions
- remediation guidance
- severity distribution
- downloadable examiner report

Initial focus areas:
- IT metrics validation
- vendor / SOC 2 reliance review
- privacy and customer data evidence review
- SDLC and AI governance evidence review

This is not intended to replace professional judgment. It is intended to make assurance review sharper, faster, and more defensible.

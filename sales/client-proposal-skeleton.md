# Sentinel v0.7 Client Proposal Skeleton

## Engagement Title

Sentinel Evidence & Metrics Assurance Review

## Objective

Assess whether selected evidence packages, metrics, vendor review artifacts, privacy records, SDLC evidence, or AI governance records are defensible under audit, 2LOD, and examiner-style scrutiny.

## Scope Options

### Option 1 — Focused Evidence Health Check

- 1 evidence package
- Up to 10 artifacts
- Sentinel examiner report
- Finding register
- Remediation evidence request list

### Option 2 — Multi-Domain Assurance Review

- 3 to 5 evidence packages
- Coverage across metrics, vendor, privacy, SDLC, or AI governance
- Executive summary
- Finding register
- Management response support
- Remediation roadmap

### Option 3 — Monthly Assurance Support

- Recurring evidence challenge
- Monthly finding register
- Governance report support
- Committee-ready summary
- Control evidence improvement support

## Deliverables

1. Sentinel Examiner Report
2. Finding Register CSV
3. Evidence Gap Summary
4. Examiner Challenge Question Bank
5. Remediation Evidence Request List
6. Executive Summary
7. Management Response Notes

## Timeline

Focused review: 3 to 5 business days  
Multi-domain review: 1 to 3 weeks  
Monthly support: recurring cadence
